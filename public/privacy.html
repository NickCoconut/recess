Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

This policy explains what information Recess (recessgame.com) collects, why, and what choices you have. Recess is operated from Canada.

1. What we collect

  • Email address — only if you create an account. We use it to send you a one-time sign-in code and to identify your account. Recess uses passwordless sign-in, so we never store a password.
  • Your writing — the sentences and free-writes you submit, along with the date, mode, and whether you chose to share them publicly.
  • Approximate location and weather — a coarse, city-level location and current weather, used to set the ambient mood and the optional context line shown with shared sentences. This is not precise GPS tracking.
  • Basic technical data — standard information your browser sends, used to keep the Service running and secure.

2. How we use it

  • To operate Recess — show you today’s theme, save your writing, run the feed.
  • To send sign-in codes to your email.
  • To generate your vibe readout.
  • To keep the public feed free of harmful content.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how Recess is used so we can improve it.

We do not sell your personal information. We don’t show ads that track you.

3. Service providers

We use trusted third parties to run Recess. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and storage.
  • Vercel — hosting and delivery.
  • Resend — sending your sign-in code emails.
  • Anthropic — generating themes and vibe readouts from your text.
  • OpenAI — automated content-safety checks (if enabled).
  • Cloudflare — DNS, security, and contact email routing.
  • OpenWeatherMap — weather data for your area.

Some of these providers may process data outside Canada (for example, in the United States). By using Recess, you understand your information may be processed in other countries.

4. Your writing and the public feed

Only writing you explicitly choose to share is shown in the public daily feed, and only the sentence itself (plus the optional ambient line) — never your email, and never your private vibe readout. Anything you keep private stays private to your account.

5. Your choices and rights

You can use Recess anonymously without giving an email at all. If you have an account, you can request to access, correct, or delete your personal information by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as Canada’s PIPEDA or the EU’s GDPR, including the right to withdraw consent.

6. Data retention

We keep your writing and account data for as long as your account is active. Anonymous data that is never linked to an account may be cleaned up periodically. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data.

7. Children

Recess is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We’ll update the date above when we do.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email hello@recessgame.com.
